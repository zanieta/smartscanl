Manual discovery only. Click Discover devices to check the approved ranges. Refresh results does not scan.


This uses active TCP discovery probes, not passive monitoring or a vulnerability scan. Firewalls can hide devices; absence does not mean a device is offline. Times below are UTC.


Discovery uses local gateway/neighbor information, offline MAC-vendor data and up to 32 hostname lookups per range through the server's configured name service. Device types are hints, not authenticated identities. Private MAC addresses, sleeping devices and isolated networks limit identification. Discovery itself never attempts login; the optional approved login check is a separate action.
